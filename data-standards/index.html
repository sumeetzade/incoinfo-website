Our data integrity and filing protocol

Where the data comes from, how a report is compiled, and what happens when a company files late.

Official source filings

IncoInfo processes, structures and compiles company financial profiles directly from official regulatory filings with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the Registrar of Companies, tax registries and statutory charge declarations.

Figures are taken from filings on record, and ratios are calculated from those filed figures. Nothing is estimated.

How orders are compiled

{{icon:file-check-2}}

Compiled when you order

When you select and buy a company, our system compiles the latest statutory documents available in the public domain for that company.

{{icon:clock}}

Dependent on regulatory filings

Reports reflect data up to the most recent filing period the company has submitted. If it has delayed its filings, you receive the complete verified data up to its latest filing on record.

{{icon:refresh-cw}}

12-month subscription included

If the company files new financial statements or changes its board or charges within 12 months of your purchase, your report is refreshed at no extra credit cost.

Data quality and privacy

We rely strictly on verified regulatory records, statutory filings and public corporate disclosures. All data is processed in line with applicable data privacy regulations and security guidelines.

  • Payments are handled by a PCI-compliant payment gateway. We do not store card details.
  • Reports and account data are encrypted in transit.
  • Personal information about directors is limited to what appears in public statutory filings.
