01 · The whole universe

Not a sample. Every company ever registered with the MCA.

Most databases start and end with active firms. incoinfo covers all 31,92,605 registered companies — including the 9,01,936 struck off and 40,869 amalgamated — so your history and due diligence never hit a gap.

Two in every three registered companies, 21.55 lakh, are active today.

02 · Structure

Private enterprise is the backbone of corporate India.

Over 92% of registered companies are private. Public companies number 1,54,313, and 1,00,447 One Person Companies show the rise of the solo founder.

Combine structure with state, sector and capital to answer questions like how many active private companies in Gujarat have paid-up capital above ₹10 crore?

03 · Markets

Nine in ten active public companies are unlisted.

Of the 75,056 public companies active today, just 7,385 (9.8%) are listed and 67,671 (90.2%) are unlisted — outside exchange disclosures, exactly where incoinfo's coverage matters most.

Across all statuses, 1,54,313 public companies are on record: 9,372 listed and 1,44,941 unlisted.

04 · Legal form

Every legal structure, on record.

Beyond 31,55,090 companies limited by shares, we cover 36,524 limited by guarantee and 991 unlimited companies.

Liability framework travels with structure, status and capital, so a guarantee company is never mistaken for a company limited by shares.

06 · Sectors

Nine in ten companies sit in just ten sectors.

Business Services is the largest of the 20 industry sectors, with 8,05,050 companies — 25.22% of all 31,92,605 records. The top ten sectors together cover 89.09% of the register, across all company statuses.

Manufacturing arrives split across two classifications, and agriculture and allied activities number 1,26,977 — so a sector filter is rarely one click, and incoinfo resolves it for you.
