What a company report contains

We process and index official documents filed with regulatory authorities to give you a clear, objective view of any business operating in India. Every report follows the same eight sections, so companies are easy to compare.

{{icon:file-spreadsheet}}

Financial statements

Audited balance sheets, profit-and-loss statements and cash flows, compiled from annual filings.

AOC-4 filings, auditor’s notes, schedules

{{icon:chart-column}}

Balance sheet & P&L

Three to five years laid side by side in one standard format, ready for Excel and valuation models.

Revenue, EBITDA, net profit, assets, reserves

{{icon:chart-line}}

Ratios & credit indicators

The ratios lenders and analysts check first, calculated the same way for every company.

Debt-to-equity, interest cover, margins, working capital days

{{icon:users}}

Directors & shareholding

Current and former directors, their other directorships, and who owns the company.

DIN, appointment dates, promoter and external holdings

{{icon:landmark}}

Charges & borrowings

Registered charges, mortgages and bank loans, to assess what the company owes and to whom.

Lender, amount, creation date, open or satisfied

{{icon:file-check-2}}

Compliance & filings

Whether annual filings are on time, and whether the GST registration is active.

AOC-4 and MGT-7 dates, GST status, registered address

{{icon:gavel}}

Legal cases

Cases involving the company, where they appear in public court and tribunal records.

Forum, case type, status, filing date

{{icon:network}}

Group & related companies

Parent and subsidiary companies, and sister concerns linked through common directors.

Holding structure, related-party disclosures

How ordering works

Search is free. You choose the companies, pay with credits, and we compile each report from the latest filings on record.

  1. 1

    Search and shortlist

    Find companies by name, CIN, director, location, sector or revenue band, and add the ones you want to your basket.

  2. 2

    Pay with credits

    Use credits from a package you already hold, or buy a pack sized to the number of companies you need.

  3. 3

    We compile the report

    Our system fetches, checks and formats the company’s latest filings into a structured report in your account.

  4. 4

    Download and stay updated

    Download PDF and Excel files from your dashboard or pull them by API. New filings are added for 12 months.
