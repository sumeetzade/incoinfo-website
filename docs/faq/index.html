Reports and delivery

12 months of access to the company’s compiled data: multi-year financial statements, directors and shareholding, registered charges, compliance status and related companies. You can download the statutory filings based on your package, and you get refreshed data whenever a scheduled refresh happens during those 12 months.

Our delivery norms depend on the compilation level in your plan: Standard (Professional) is up to 25 companies a week per client, Expedited (Business and Corporate) is up to 50 companies a week per client, and Priority (Enterprise) is up to 100 companies a week per client. Depending on workload, our standard terms allow these norms to vary. Our delivery team will confirm the timeline when we receive your order.
