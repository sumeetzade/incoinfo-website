What a company report contains

We process and index official documents filed with regulatory authorities to give you a clear, objective view of any registered business. Every report follows the same eight sections, so companies are easy to compare.

Financial statements

Audited balance sheets, profit-and-loss statements and cash flows, compiled from annual filings.

Annual filings, auditor’s notes, schedules

Balance sheet & P&L

Three to five years laid side by side in one standard format, ready for Excel and valuation models.

Revenue, EBITDA, net profit, assets, reserves

Ratios & credit indicators

The ratios lenders and analysts check first, calculated the same way for every company.

Debt-to-equity, interest cover, margins, working capital days

Directors & shareholding

Current and former directors, their other directorships, and who owns the company.

Director IDs, appointment dates, promoter and external holdings

Charges & borrowings

Registered charges, mortgages and bank loans, to assess what the company owes and to whom.

Lender, amount, creation date, open or satisfied

Compliance & filings

Whether annual filings are on time, and whether the tax registration is active.

Annual filing dates, tax status, registered address

Legal cases

Cases involving the company, where they appear in public court and tribunal records.

Forum, case type, status, filing date

Group & related companies

Parent and subsidiary companies, and sister concerns linked through common directors.

Holding structure, related-party disclosures
