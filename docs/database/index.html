Data covered in each profile

Some fields depend on what the company has filed. Reports cover data up to its latest filing on record.

Data categories and the information included in each
Data categoryInformation included
Basic entity profileLegal name, CIN, incorporation date, state and RoC jurisdiction, company category (private, public or LLP), active or inactive status, registered office address.
Financial performanceThree to five years of revenue, EBITDA, net profit, debtor and creditor balances, total assets, reserves and operating cash flows.
Capital and ownershipAuthorised capital, paid-up capital, shareholding pattern, promoter vs external shareholders, equity class splits.
Management and boardCurrent and past directors, Director Identification Numbers (DIN), appointment dates, other directorships held.
Charges and liabilitiesSecured loans, lenders (banks and NBFCs), charge amounts, date of charge creation, charge status (open or satisfied).
Tax and filingsActive GST registrations, annual return filing dates, balance sheet filing compliance status.
