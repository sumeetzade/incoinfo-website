Data disclaimer

How to interpret information in incoinfo reports.

incoinfo compiles information from public and official sources, including company registry filings (in India, our current coverage: Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Registrar of Companies), tax registrations and registered charge records.

Reports are provided for reference and due diligence. They reflect each company’s filings up to its latest submission on record and are not investment, credit or legal advice. Verify material facts independently before acting on them.
